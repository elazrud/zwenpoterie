Important Workshop Details

Drying Time, Raw (Brute) Clay & Optional Coloring

Important: The pieces you make will be raw (brute) pottery and will be ready for pick-up in approximately 3 days. If you wish to have your pieces colored and kiln-glazed, this artisanal finishing process costs 30 DH and takes an extra 2 to 3 days. Coloring options and details can be chosen directly during your session or on WhatsApp.

Standard Raw (Brute) Pottery:

Natural matte terracotta finish. Requires a natural, gentle air-drying cycle of approx 3 days in our studio to prevent cracking before pickup.

Optional Coloring & Kiln Glazing (+30 DH):

Have your pieces painted with mineral oxides and fired in our kiln in your preferred colors for just 30 DH. This special finishing process takes an additional 2 to 3 days.

Departing Agadir before 3 days?

If your stay in Agadir finishes before your pieces have finished their natural drying cycle, simply let us know at the start of your workshop! We can safely swap your creations for already-fired studio pieces of identical style from our showroom—and you only pay the 30 DH coloring fee for it! Or, we can arrange secure packaging and dispatch to your hotel.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our authentic pottery workshop experience in Agadir.

Do I need any previous pottery experience?
Not at all! Over 90% of our workshop guests have never touched a pottery wheel before. Our master artisan begins every session with an authentic introduction explaining our fabrication steps, then gently guides your hands on the wheel so you successfully craft real pieces you'll cherish forever.
How is the workshop session structured?
Every session begins with an introduction to how we work and the traditional steps of Moroccan ceramic fabrication—from raw clay preparation to wheel dynamics. You then take your place at the traditional potter's wheel for a fully guided hands-on session where you shape and complete your pieces with continuous master guidance.
Can I book as a solo traveler, couple, or small group?
Yes, absolutely! Solo travelers, couples, families with kids, and groups of friends are all warmly welcomed. Everyone gets dedicated wheel time, personal attention, and master guidance.
When will my pieces be ready for pick-up?
Natural raw (brute) pottery requires approximately 3 days of slow, natural air drying in our studio to prevent structural cracking. If you choose our colored and kiln-glazed option (30 DH), the painting and high-temperature kiln firing take an additional 2 to 3 days.
What if my holiday in Agadir ends before 3 days?
If your stay in Agadir finishes before your creations have completed their drying cycle, simply let us know at the start of your workshop! We can safely swap your creations for already-fired studio pieces of identical style from our showroom—and you only pay the 30 DH coloring fee for it! Alternatively, we can arrange secure packaging and dispatch to your hotel.
What kind of clay and materials do you use?
We work exclusively with 100% natural, local Moroccan red clay sourced from the region. It is completely organic, non-toxic, and chemical-free. All glazes meet international health and safety standards.
What should I wear? Will my clothes get stained?
Natural Moroccan clay washes out completely with plain water and leaves no stains. We recommend casual, comfortable clothing and keeping fingernails trimmed for the best wheel grip and feel!
How do I confirm my booking and pricing?
Booking is direct and hassle-free via WhatsApp. There is no pre-payment required. Simply message us with your preferred date and workshop offer, and we will confirm your slot and details immediately.
