The Art of Clay, The Legacy of Hands

Born in Safi, Morocco in 1975 into a devoted family with a baker father, an uncle carpenter, and an older brother who was a master potter, Master El Mostafa El Azroud grew up where the air smelled of fresh bread, wood shavings, and wet clay.

At age 11, he touched clay for the first time and began his rigorous apprenticeship. Today, with over 40 years of continuous mastery, he leads our artisan cooperative in Agadir to celebrate the authentic essence of raw Moroccan ceramics and pass on his family craft.

"Give value to what you make with your own hands. Happiness lies in patience."

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting our Agadir studio or booking a workshop.

Do I need any previous pottery experience?
Not at all! Over 90% of our workshop guests have never touched a pottery wheel before. Our master artisan introduces our fabrication steps, then gently guides your hands on the wheel so you successfully craft real pieces you'll cherish forever.
When will my pieces be ready, and what if my holiday is short?
Natural raw clay requires approx 3 days of slow air drying. If your stay in Agadir finishes earlier, you can safely swap your creations for already-fired studio pieces of identical style from our showroom for just the 30 DH coloring fee, or arrange secure packaging to your hotel.
How do I reserve a workshop or order custom ceramics?
Everything is direct and easy via WhatsApp with no upfront payment required. Simply message us with your preferred date, workshop offer, or custom order ideas, and we will confirm your reservation immediately.
